Meadows Baptist Church × Hyper Fenton

It started with
“Let’s grab lunch.”

Hyper Fenton (Seth Fenton) is a music artist and pastor at Meadows Baptist Church, across the street from Plano East Senior High School. He’s always inviting people around town to grab lunch and hear their stories.

Free Lunch Tag grew out of that habit. Now Meadows hosts are extending the same invitation to their neighbors.

But… why?

We believe God is relational. Jesus made time to sit at the table with people, and we want to love our neighbors that way, too.

You’re welcome whether you come to church or simply want someone to share a meal with.
