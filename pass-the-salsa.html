Enter my tag

  1. Find your host.

    Scan the QR on the back of your tag or enter its 12-character code. You’ll see your host’s photo, a little about them, and their number.

  2. Make a lunch plan.

    Tap Text my host and suggest a time and place. Want to bring a friend? Ask your host when you make the plan.

    “Hey, I got your tag! Tacos at noon? Can a friend join?”

  3. Enjoy lunch. Keep the tag.

    After lunch, keep your design or trade with a friend. The code still points to the original host.

Can I bring someone?

Ask your host who can join and what lunch covers. If you’re under 18, arrange the meal with your parent or guardian.

Do I need to attend church?

Church attendance is optional. Seth, a pastor at Meadows, takes neighbors in Plano to lunch to hear their stories. Those meals inspired Free Lunch Tag.

Could I host a lunch?

Tell your host you’d like to buy lunch for a neighbor next time. Meadows sets up each new host with a profile and tags of their own.

Meadows Baptist Church × Hyper Fenton

It started with
“Let’s grab lunch.”

Hyper Fenton (Seth Fenton) is a music artist and pastor at Meadows Baptist Church, across the street from Plano East Senior High School. He’s always inviting people around town to grab lunch and hear their stories.

Free Lunch Tag grew out of that habit. Now Meadows hosts are extending the same invitation to their neighbors.

But… why?

We believe God is relational. Jesus made time to sit at the table with people, and we want to love our neighbors that way, too.

You’re welcome whether you come to church or simply want someone to share a meal with.
